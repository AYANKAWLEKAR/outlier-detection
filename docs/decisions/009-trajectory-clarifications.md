# 009 — Trajectory monitoring: clarifications

Status: **decided (Task 0.1, 2026-11-11).** Interpretation record for the trajectory-monitoring
feature (`docs/feature-spec-trajectory-monitoring.md`). Rows T-19 onward are added by later
tasks when a decision arises that the plan does not cover.

Paper citation: "OnTrack", arXiv:2610.12375. Licence note: paraphrase only; the paper's prose,
figures and tables are never reproduced here or in code. Nothing in the codebase is named
"OnTrack" or "OTAP".

## Scope and interpretation

Branch `feat/trajectory-monitoring`, cut from `feat/tiers-0-2` at `2aafb11`. Tiers 0–2 exist; Tier 3 (blast radius, §6.9) and Tier 4 (Go proxy, Redis, enforcement) do not. The plan builds everything the feature spec places in Tiers 0–3 in Python, plus the Python-proxy parts of F3 and F2.

| T-27 | Vital-signs details the brief leaves open (Task 1.5). **Ring:** `RECENT_MAX = 32` completed calls per session in the tracker with their response fingerprints; `SessionTracker.dump/load` round-trip both (a dump without them loads as an empty ring). `delta_i` of a call with a fingerprint but no fingerprinted call before it is 1.0 (everything is new). `new_artifacts` = first sightings the call's `ArtifactLedger.record` made (exact entries and Bloom adds; task-input seeds are not the call's), returned by `record`. **Spin includes the current call:** `normalize` also computes the call's own `delta_i` against the ring and carries it as `ActionEnvelope.delta_i` (a third new field, after `call_fp` and `recent`). Near-duplicates are only compared with calls of the same tool in the loop window: the best one by plain set Jaccard of `arg_tokens`. A near-duplicate of an errored call scores 0, because it is a corrected retry; it would score 0.7 at most anyway, which never raises the `LOOP` flag.
